The Middle East Children’s Alliance (MECA) Community App is being developed by the CFTC team to strengthen MECA’s ability to connect with its global network of supporters, donors, and activists. The app serves as a hub for engagement, offering access to events, stories, fundraising campaigns, and shopping opportunities—all designed to empower MECA’s community and expand the organization’s impact. Built over a structured 12-week development cycle, the project is divided into distinct phases to ensure timely delivery of core features.

Within this project, my contributions centered on building essential user-facing features and ensuring smooth integration with MECA’s existing systems. I developed the Shop page in React Native, connecting it to product APIs while also designing a fallback interface for cases where access to certain data was restricted. To keep the community informed in real time, I automated a blog-to-notification pipeline using Python and GitHub Actions, enabling MECA to instantly broadcast updates across the app. Additionally, I created the frontend for the Fundraising page and successfully integrated donation APIs, navigating limited permissions to ensure that campaign metrics remained accurate and reliable.

Through these contributions, I helped deliver key functionality that empowers MECA to better engage its community, streamline supporter interactions, and amplify its mission of supporting children and families in the Middle East.
