The Middle East Children’s Alliance (MECA) - a nonprofit founded in 1988 that provides humanitarian aid, education, and cultural support for children and families in the Middle East - is partnering with the CFTC team on the MECA Community App to strengthen its ability to connect with its global network of supporters, donors, and activists. The app serves as a hub for engagement, offering access to events, stories, fundraising campaigns, and shopping opportunities. Built over a structured 12-week development cycle, the project is divided into distinct phases to ensure timely delivery of core features.

Within this project, my contributions centered on building essential user-facing features and ensuring smooth integration with MECA’s existing systems. I developed the Shop page in React Native, working closely with a teammate who built GraphQL query endpoints that powered product data retrieval. I integrated these queries into the frontend, ensuring smooth communication between the app and MECA’s backend systems. To maintain reliability, I also designed a fallback interface for cases where access to certain data was restricted, so users could still browse and interact with the storefront without disruption.
To keep the community informed in real time, I automated a blog-to-notification pipeline using Python and GitHub Actions, enabling MECA to instantly broadcast stories announces to users around the world. Additionally, I created the frontend for the Fundraising page and successfully integrated donation APIs, navigating limited permissions to ensure that campaign metrics remained accurate and reliable.

Through these contributions, I helped deliver key functionality that empowers MECA to better engage its community, streamline supporter interactions, and amplify its mission of supporting children and families in the Middle East.
